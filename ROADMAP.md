# Seentics Roadmap

## What We've Built
Analytics platform for real-time tracking and behavior analysis. Core features are working - event tracking, site management, and real-time analytics dashboard.

---

## Feature Roadmap

### 📧 Email & Communication
- **Automated Email Support** - Send behavioral-triggered emails to customers
- **Email Campaign Management** - Track and manage internal customer emails
- **Email Service Integrations** - Connect with popular providers like Mailchimp and ConvertKit

### 🎟️ Support & Success
- **Integrated Support Desk** - Manage customer queries and support tickets directly in Seentics
- **Live Chat Widget** - Real-time customer support capabilities
- **Knowledge Base Builder** - Help customers help themselves with integrated documentation

### 📝 Customer Engagement
- **Dynamic Forms** - ✅ Email capture forms inside popups (custom fields, consent, thank-you message); standalone embeddable forms are still planned
- **Survey Engine** - Trigger NPS and satisfaction surveys based on user behavior
- **Lead Capture Tools** - ✅ Leads inbox with search, status, CSV export and per-popup/variant attribution. Still planned: spin-the-wheel, countdown, multi-step and sticky-bar templates, coupon codes, and email/CRM integrations (Mailchimp, Klaviyo, HubSpot, Zapier)

### 🎨 Frontend & UI
- **Dashboard Polish** - Premium layout refinements and advanced data visualization
- **Mobile Experience** - Fully responsive mission-critical dashboards
- **Design System** - Consistent, high-end aesthetics across all modules

### 📈 Advanced Analytics
- **Cohort Analysis** - Deep dives into user retention and long-term behavior
- **Custom KPIs** - User-defined conversion goals and success metrics
- **Data Export** - Enterprise-grade CSV/JSON data portability

### 🔌 Integrations
- **Platform Marketplace** - Seamless connection with e-commerce and CRM tools
- **Zapier & Webhooks** - Extensive automation capabilities for external workflows

### 🛡️ Security & Privacy
- **Enterprise-Grade Security** - 2FA, advanced audit logs, and role-based access
- **Full Compliance** - Tools for maintaining GDPR/CCPA standards
