# Poptin Alternatives: 9 Popup and Lead Capture Tools Compared (2026)

<img src="/blog/poptin-alternatives/logo-poptin.png" alt="Poptin logo" width="40" height="40" />

Poptin is a well-reviewed popup and form builder. On Capterra it holds a 4.8 rating from several hundred reviews, and reviewers praise ease of use and support. So if you are searching for a Poptin alternative, you probably are not escaping a broken product. The reasons are usually more specific: pricing sized by visitors, a wish to see what happens after a popup is shown, a privacy review, or a desire to stop adding another third-party script.

This guide compares nine options (Popupsmart, Poper, OptinMonster, Privy, Wisepops, your email platform's own forms, a DIY modal, Seentics and Poptin itself as the baseline), explains who each one fits, and ends with a migration checklist. Comparisons are as of October 2026. Prices are what third-party and vendor pages listed when we checked, and they change, so confirm them on the vendor's own page.

## TL;DR

- **Want a like-for-like popup builder:** Popupsmart or Poper match Poptin's template-and-form approach.
- **Want a mature ecosystem with deep targeting:** OptinMonster.
- **Run an online store with email and SMS:** Privy.
- **Want popups tied to real behavior and measurable results, with optional self-hosting:** Seentics.
- **Be clear about the gap:** Seentics now captures emails in popups and keeps them in a Leads inbox, but it does not yet have a drag-and-drop visual builder, a large template library, spin-the-wheel or countdown popups, or native email-platform integrations. If your main job is polished, template-driven email capture with direct Mailchimp or Klaviyo sync, a dedicated tool fits better today.
- **Test with one campaign** on one page for two weeks before migrating everything.

## Why people look beyond Poptin

### 1. Pricing that scales with visitors

Poptin's free plan is listed at 1,000 visitors a month on one domain. [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/165478/Poptin/pricing/) lists Basic at $25 per month (10,000 visitors), Pro at $59 (50,000 visitors, 4 domains) and an Agency tier at $119 (150,000 visitors); another source says those prices assume annual billing. We could not load poptin.com itself, so check the current figures there.

The structural point matters more than any number. When the plan is sized by site visitors or pageviews, a good traffic month can push you up a tier even if the popup is only shown to a small share of those visitors.

### 2. Popups in a silo

A popup tool tells you how many people saw a message and how many submitted. It rarely tells you what they did next, which campaign brought them or whether they later bought. That takes analytics, funnels and sometimes session replay, usually in another product. Our [conversion rate optimization guide](/blog/conversion-rate-optimization-with-analytics) covers why that join matters.

### 3. One more third-party script

Every widget adds a script and a data processor. Teams that minimize processors or want data in their own environment find consolidation attractive. See [privacy-first analytics](/blog/privacy-first-analytics).

### 4. Feature fit

Capterra reviewers mention a learning curve, some confusing menus (A/B testing in particular), and a wish for more integrations and better mobile exit intent. These are mild complaints, not failures, but they show where a replacement could differ.

## What to check in any replacement

| Question | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| What sizes the plan (visitors, pageviews, contacts)? | Determines how the bill reacts to growth. |
| Which triggers exist (exit intent, scroll, time, click, returning visitor)? | Targeting quality decides whether popups help or annoy. |
| Are mobile triggers supported? | Cursor-based exit intent does not exist on phones. |
| Where do leads go (inbox, email platform, webhook)? | Submissions must reach your CRM or list. |
| Can you measure what happens after the popup? | Without it you see form fills, not business impact. |
| Is consent handled? | Collecting emails needs a lawful basis and a clear notice. |
| Is there a free tier or trial? | Lets you test with real traffic first. |

## The 9 options

### 1. Poptin (the baseline)

<img src="/blog/poptin-alternatives/logo-poptin.png" alt="Poptin logo" width="32" height="32" />

[Poptin](https://www.poptin.com/) is a popup, form and lead capture builder with templates, targeting and integrations. Free plan: 1,000 visitors per month and one domain. Paid per Capterra: $25, $59 and $119 per month, with the lower figures tied to annual billing.

**Best for:** marketers who want a fast visual builder and a leads list.
**Trade-off:** pricing follows visitors, and analytics beyond form results live elsewhere.

### 2. Popupsmart

<img src="/blog/poptin-alternatives/logo-popupsmart.png" alt="Popupsmart logo" width="32" height="32" />

[Popupsmart](https://popupsmart.com/) is a dedicated popup builder with templates, targeting rules and form fields, and it publishes its own Poptin comparison. Its [pricing page](https://popupsmart.com/pricing) lists a free plan with 5,000 pageviews per month (one live popup, one website), then Basic at $39 per month (50K pageviews), Advanced at $69 (100K), Pro at $99 (250K) and Expert at $159 (500K), with 20% off annual billing.

**Best for:** teams that want the same style of tool at a different price.
**Trade-off:** still a standalone widget, and its own comparison pages are promotional.

### 3. Poper

<img src="/blog/poptin-alternatives/logo-poper.png" alt="Poper logo" width="32" height="32" />

[Poper](https://www.poper.ai/pricing) lists a free plan with 10,000 monthly pageviews, one website, 100 leads and branded popups, with no A/B testing or team invites on free. Its marketing pages quote paid plans starting around $15 per month, though the pricing page shows a different figure, so confirm before budgeting.

**Best for:** small sites that want a cheap, simple popup tool.
**Trade-off:** check advanced targeting and integrations against your needs.

### 4. OptinMonster

<img src="/blog/poptin-alternatives/logo-optinmonster.png" alt="OptinMonster logo" width="32" height="32" />

[OptinMonster](https://optinmonster.com/) is a long-established lead capture platform with deep targeting and many integrations. Third-party sources list Basic, Plus, Pro and Growth plans from about $7 to $49 per month on annual billing, but one review says those are introductory rates and renewals are much higher (for example Pro around $72.50 per month on annual billing). Pro covers up to 3 sites and 25,000 monthly impressions; Growth covers 5 sites and 100,000. Treat these as unconfirmed until you read the current pricing page.

**Best for:** publishers and marketers who want a mature ecosystem.
**Trade-off:** renewal pricing and impression limits need a close read.

### 5. Privy

<img src="/blog/poptin-alternatives/logo-privy.png" alt="Privy logo" width="32" height="32" />

[Privy](https://privy.com/pricing) targets online stores with popups, email and SMS. Its pricing page lists Starter at $30 per month (up to 1,500 contacts) and Growth at $45 (adds SMS), and its help center describes a free tier for accounts under 100 mailable contacts, with Privy branding.

**Best for:** Shopify-style stores that want popups, email flows and SMS in one subscription.
**Trade-off:** priced by contacts, and built around the commerce workflow.

### 6. Wisepops

<img src="/blog/poptin-alternatives/logo-wisepops.png" alt="Wisepops logo" width="32" height="32" />

[Wisepops](https://wisepops.com/) offers popups, bars and on-site messages. Published prices conflict across sources: Capterra lists plans from $49 per month (50K pageviews) up to $499, while Shopify App Store and competitor pages cite higher figures for larger traffic tiers. It offers a free trial but no free plan on Capterra.

**Best for:** teams that need richer on-site messaging and are comfortable with traffic-tiered pricing.
**Trade-off:** confirm the exact price for your pageviews with the vendor.

### 7. Your email platform's own forms

<img src="/blog/poptin-alternatives/logo-mailchimp.png" alt="Mailchimp logo" width="32" height="32" /> <img src="/blog/poptin-alternatives/logo-klaviyo.png" alt="Klaviyo logo" width="32" height="32" />

Mailchimp, Klaviyo, ConvertKit and others ship popup forms that write directly to your list.

**Best for:** teams already living in one email tool.
**Trade-off:** targeting is usually basic, and results sit apart from site analytics.

### 8. Build it yourself

A small modal, a form posting to your backend, and a few lines of JavaScript. Fine for one placement, painful to maintain across many.

**Best for:** engineering teams with one or two placements.
**Trade-off:** no targeting UI, A/B testing or reporting unless you build it.

## Migration checklist

1. **List every live popup.** Note its trigger, audience, page and goal. Most accounts have a few popups that produce nearly all the value.
2. **Export your leads** and confirm they are in your email platform or CRM before switching anything off.
3. **Sort popups into two groups:** those that need a form builder (keep or move to a form tool) and behavior-triggered messages (rebuild as automations).
4. **Define a conversion event** for each, so you measure impact and not only submissions.
5. **Check consent and notices** for any personal data you collect.
6. **Run in parallel** on one page for one to two weeks.
7. **Measure page performance** with each script on and off using your own tooling.
8. **Remove the old script** from every template and tag manager only after the parallel run confirms nothing is missing.

## FAQ

### What is the best Poptin alternative?

It depends on the job. For a template-driven builder, Popupsmart, Poper or OptinMonster. For stores, Privy. For behavior-triggered messages tied to analytics and replays, Seentics.

### Is there a free Poptin alternative?

Several tools have free tiers with visitor, pageview or contact caps (Poper, Popupsmart, Privy). Seentics is free to self-host; you pay for your own infrastructure.

### Does Seentics replace Poptin completely?

Not yet. It covers behavior-triggered popups and banners with email capture, a Leads inbox and measurement, but not Poptin's visual builder, template library, gamified formats or native email integrations. See [Poptin vs Seentics](/blog/poptin-vs-seentics).

### Do popups hurt SEO?

Intrusive full-screen interstitials on mobile can be penalized by search engines, so prefer small, delayed or exit-triggered formats and test on mobile.

### Can I use popups and stay GDPR compliant?

It depends on what you collect: lawful basis or consent, clear notices and processor agreements. Talk to your legal counsel; this article is not legal advice. See [GDPR analytics without a cookie banner](/blog/gdpr-analytics-without-cookie-banner).
