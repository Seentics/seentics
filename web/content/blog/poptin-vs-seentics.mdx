# Poptin vs Seentics: Popups, Automations and Analytics Compared (2026)

<img src="/blog/poptin-alternatives/logo-poptin.png" alt="Poptin logo" width="48" height="48" /> &nbsp;**vs**&nbsp; <img src="/blog/poptin-alternatives/logo-seentics.svg" alt="Seentics logo" width="48" height="48" />

These two products overlap, but they start from opposite ends. **Poptin** is a popup and form builder: templates, form fields, a leads list and integrations. **Seentics** is privacy-first analytics (funnels, heatmaps, session replay, revenue) with behavioral automations that can show modals and banners. We build Seentics, so read this with that in mind. We say plainly where Poptin is the better choice.

Poptin details come from public sources such as [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/165478/Poptin/) because we could not load poptin.com directly. Confirm current features and pricing on the vendor's site. Comparisons are as of October 2026.

## TL;DR

- **Poptin wins** at building campaigns fast: templates, form fields, a leads list and email integrations.
- **Seentics wins** at targeting from real behavior, measuring what happened next (funnels, goals, replays, revenue), privacy and self-hosting.
- **Seentics does not fully replace Poptin yet.** It captures emails and keeps them in a Leads inbox, but has no visual drag-and-drop builder, large template library, gamified formats or native email integrations.
- **You can use both:** a form tool for email capture and Seentics for analytics and behavior-triggered messages.

## Pricing

Poptin's free plan is listed at 1,000 visitors per month and one domain, with paid plans per [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/165478/Poptin/pricing/) at $25 (10,000 visitors, 1,000 autoresponders), $59 (50,000 visitors, 4 domains) and $119 (150,000 visitors). One source says those prices require annual billing. Seentics offers a Free plan and a Pro plan with optional metered extra usage, and you can self-host the open-source core at no license cost (you pay for your own infrastructure). See the [Seentics pricing page](/pricing).

The practical difference: Poptin charges for the popup tool, so a site that also needs analytics, heatmaps and replay pays for those separately. Seentics bundles them, but gives you less popup tooling.

## Where Poptin is better

- **Building campaigns fast.** A visual editor, templates and form fields mean a marketer can ship a popup in minutes without developer help.
- **Collecting and routing leads.** A leads list and email-platform integrations are built in.
- **Popup-specific features** such as A/B testing and countdown or gamified formats.
- **Proven reception.** Capterra shows a 4.8 rating from about 345 reviews, with ease of use and customer service rated highly. Complaints are mild: a learning curve, some confusing menus (A/B testing in particular), and a wish for more integrations and better mobile exit intent.

## Where Seentics is better

- **Popups that respond to real behavior.** Show a message only to returning visitors from a paid campaign who scrolled 75% of the pricing page, using data the tracker already collects. See the [behavioral automations guide](/blog/behavioral-automations-guide).
- **Knowing whether it worked.** The same product holds [funnels](/blog/funnel-analysis-guide), goals and revenue, so you can see whether visitors who got the message converted, and watch [session replays](/blog/session-replay-guide) of what they did next.
- **Heatmaps for placement decisions.** See where people click and scroll before choosing where a banner belongs. See [heatmaps explained](/blog/heatmaps-explained).
- **Privacy and ownership.** No cookies or fingerprinting, one tracker script, and the option to self-host. See [privacy-first analytics](/blog/privacy-first-analytics).
- **Consolidation.** One tool and one processor instead of separate analytics, replay and popup products.

## Example: the same job in each tool

**Goal:** show a free-trial offer to first-time visitors who read most of the pricing page and try to leave.

- **In Poptin:** pick an exit-intent template, set the page and new-visitor rules, add an email field and connect your email platform. Results show views and submissions.
- **In Seentics:** create an automation with an exit-intent trigger on the pricing page and conditions for first-time visitors, then add a modal with the email form switched on. Leads land in the Leads inbox, attributed to the popup and variant. Add a goal on the `lead_captured` event and open the funnel to compare visitors who saw the message with those who did not.

Both work. The first is faster to build. The second is easier to evaluate.

## Switching from Poptin

1. List your live popups and their triggers, audiences and goals.
2. Export your Poptin leads and import them into your email platform; new Seentics leads can be exported as CSV.
3. Decide which popups need a form builder (keep these in a form tool) and which are really behavior-triggered messages (rebuild these as automations).
4. Create a goal for each so you measure outcomes, not only submissions.
5. Run both in parallel on one page, then remove the old script only when nothing is missing.

More options are in [Poptin alternatives](/blog/poptin-alternatives).

## FAQ

### Is Seentics a Poptin alternative?

For behavior-triggered popups with email capture, a Leads inbox and measurement, yes. For a template-driven visual builder with native email integrations, not yet.

### Can Seentics collect email addresses?

Yes. A modal can carry an email form with optional extra fields and a consent checkbox. Submissions go to a built-in Leads inbox that you can search, export as CSV or delete from. There is no native Mailchimp, Klaviyo or HubSpot sync yet; export a CSV or use a webhook.

### Does Seentics need a cookie banner?

Seentics uses no cookies and no fingerprinting, storing one visitor id in `localStorage`. Whether you need a notice for data your popups collect depends on what you collect. Ask your legal counsel; this is not legal advice. See [GDPR analytics without a cookie banner](/blog/gdpr-analytics-without-cookie-banner).

### Which is cheaper?

It depends on traffic and needs. Poptin prices by monthly visitors; Seentics is free to self-host or has Free and Pro plans. Compare against your own traffic on each pricing page, and count any extra analytics tools you would otherwise pay for.

### Can I run both?

Yes. Many teams keep a form tool for email capture and use Seentics for analytics and behavior-triggered messages.
